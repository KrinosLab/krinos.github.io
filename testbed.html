List projects

Translating from gene expression to model parameters Transcripts to traits

Using community gene expression data for microbial eukaryotes, the goal of this project is to recover custom, taxon-specific “traditional” model parameters

resource project
Time series gene expression data for coastal phytoplankton dynamics Water quality monitoring at high throughput

Time-series environmental data coupled with gene expression allows us to identify genes linked to phytoplankton resilience to challenging environmental conditions, and contextualize ecological shifts that impact water quality

project
Salton Sea microbial ecology At the intersection of public health and environmental research

The Salton Sea is a large, hypersaline lake (CA) with impaired water quality. Via biogeochemical models and ongoing measurements, we are studying ecological dynamics that contribute to public health hazards

project
Multiscale modeling Linking genome-scale to the water column

Work in collaboration with the C-CoMP NSF STC to develop a coupling between a genome-scale metabolic model (GEM/FBA) and a 1-D water column biogeochemical model and hence link complex biology to oceanographic dynamics

software project
Intraspecific diversity Coccolithophores and beyond

Studying intraspecific diversity in important phytoplankton groups can be used as a proxy for current model-relevant diversity & future capacity for resilience to changing environmental conditions

project
Improving protistan taxonomic annotations Databases and software tools

Via tools like EUKulele and tax-aliquots, developing openly-available software and workflows to improve the fidelity of protistan taxonomic annotations

software
Models that predict protistan trophy In the euphotic zone and at hydrothermal vents

Identifying -omics-based indicators to predict whether a microbe is more likely a heterotroph, a phototroph, or both

project resource
Post-hurricane microbial ecology Disturbance ecology and harmful algal blooms

Opportunistic sampling after Hurricanes Helene and Milton in Tampa Bay (2024) to study microbial ecological impacts of disturbance. Funding from Phycological Society of America Grants In Aid of Research (GIAR) program

project resource
Diatom blooms in the North Bering Sea Harmful algal blooms in vulnerable coastal ecosystems

Augmenting existing efforts to study impacts of stratification in the North Bering Sea with environmental -omics data. Funding from North Pacific Research Board
