# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-wrench" %}Projects

Projects in the OPAL Group center on **ecosystem impacts of taxonomic diversity**, **effects of environmental stressors on eukaryotic microbes**, and **identifying trait relationships that can be encoded into models**. These objective are achieved using a combination of -omics data collection and analysis, phytoplankton culturing in the lab, and coastal environmental fieldwork.
